Massive Attack make their return at Rock Werchter

28 October 2026

Massive Attack are making their return to Rock Werchter. Their last appearance at the festival was on 27 June 2003.

The Bristol collective joins the bill of Rock Werchter 2027, which takes place from Thursday 1 to Sunday 4 July at the Festivalpark in Werchter.

Combi tickets go on sale on Friday 30 October at 10am via the official website.
